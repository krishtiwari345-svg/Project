The workspace / Sample collection

A little structure
for everyday work.

Bring the task, the outcome, and the reflection into one place. Browse these fictional entries to see what a useful record can contain.

01 / Study

Working through a difficult topic

Focus: Explain a concept without looking at the notes.

Observation: The examples were familiar, but explaining the reasoning revealed a gap.

Next time: Write a short explanation before checking the source.

02 / Project

Making a first draft easier to review

Focus: Finish the outline of a small project.

Observation: Separating open questions from completed sections made the draft easier to discuss.

Next time: Keep a short list of decisions that still need to be made.

03 / Practice

Returning to the basics

Focus: Practice one skill with fewer interruptions.

Observation: A shorter session left enough time to review mistakes while they were still fresh.

Next time: Reserve the final five minutes for a brief reflection.
