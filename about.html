About the idea

A record you can
come back to.

Fieldnotes is a basic concept for a work journal: a place to organize activity, reflect on results, and carry useful observations into the next attempt.

Why keep more than the outcome?

A finished task tells you that something was completed. It may not tell you which approach helped, where you became stuck, or what you would change. Keeping a little context makes a record more useful later.

This approach can fit study, personal projects, skill practice, or other work that benefits from regular review. The categories may change; the habit of recording and reflecting stays useful.

What this version includes

The sample workspace shows how entries could be organized. The review page offers questions for looking back across those entries. All examples are fictional.

This first version is an informational website. It does not include accounts, a connected database, automated analysis, or personal data storage.
